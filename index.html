Current Research

Health-Aware Manifold Learning (HAML)

Ongoing research project

HAML investigates health-aware representation learning for Remaining Useful Life (RUL) prediction and degradation modelling in prognostics and health management.

The project focuses on learning meaningful degradation representations from multivariate time-series data while supporting robust and interpretable RUL prediction.
