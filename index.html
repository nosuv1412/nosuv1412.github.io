Current Research

Health-Aware Manifold Learning (HAML)

Ongoing research project

HAML investigates health-aware representation learning for Remaining Useful Life (RUL) prediction and degradation modelling in prognostics and health management. The project studies how degradation-aware representations can support robust, interpretable, and transferable RUL prediction from multivariate time-series data.
